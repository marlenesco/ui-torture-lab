# Code of Conduct

UI Torture Lab contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces respectful,
technical, and safe for participation.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect private, proprietary, and personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about MVP scope and repository safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
doxxing, credential disclosure, or publication of another person's private or
proprietary material is not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, project
safety, or productive collaboration. Platform-level abuse can also be reported
through GitHub's reporting tools.

Do not place sensitive details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities use
the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
