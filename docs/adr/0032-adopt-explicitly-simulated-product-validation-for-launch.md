# Adopt explicitly simulated Product Validation for launch

On 2026-10-26 the owner chose six professional-role AI reviews as the 1.0.0 Product Validation criterion because independent developer recruitment is unavailable, replacing the former requirement for six to eight human participants and participant-owned use. The review must declare its assisted desk-review method, concrete sources, per-role outcomes, unresolved problems and launch/iterate decision; it must never claim observed human comprehension, task times or adoption. This method may review synthetic artifacts before manual platform sign-off, but cannot waive Technical Validation, privacy/Restore blockers, the Public Product Contract or final Store-installed validation; actual human usability remains unverified.
