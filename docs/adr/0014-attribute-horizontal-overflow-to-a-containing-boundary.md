# Attribute horizontal overflow to a proven containing boundary

The detector formerly called Horizontal Element Overflow is named Horizontal Containment Overflow. Its Finding Subject is the nearest internal boundary whose stable before-and-after geometry proves that one or more normal-flow, untransformed descendants newly or increasingly exceed its usable horizontal bounds; those descendants remain supporting Evidence. One mechanism produces one aggregated Finding, farther-boundary duplicates are suppressed, and independently proven viewport overflow may coexist. The MVP excludes top-level viewport containment, explicit operational horizontal scrolling, out-of-flow and transformed elements, negative-margin layouts, and other ambiguous patterns; inability to isolate the boundary or distinguish Text Clipping is inconclusive.
